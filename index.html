Exchange rates

Rates are stored as USD per 1 unit of local currency, always. A pound buys more than a dollar (so GBP→USD is above 1); a Canadian dollar buys less (so CAD→USD is below 1). Anything outside that shape is the conversion applied backwards.

Period is taken from the posted date. Deferred rows are counted — never filter on release date.
